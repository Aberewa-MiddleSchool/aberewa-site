May & Vovô: A shared memory

ABANDONMENT

A bench outside the roda

VOVÔ Is anybody sitting here?

MEILIN My parents said to wait here. That was yesterday.

VOVÔ Then I’ll sit with you. My name is Maré.

MEILIN Meilin. You can have the dry end of the bench.

A NEW FAMILY

Two places at the table

VOVÔ I put a second bowl out. It’s yours tomorrow, too.

MEILIN And after tomorrow?

VOVÔ After tomorrow you can wash it. You live here, May.

MEILIN All right, Vovô. But your bowl looks dirtier.

GROWING UP

Saturday at the studio

VOVÔ Who tied my shoelaces together?

MEILIN Someone who wanted to see a very short ginga.

VOVÔ Undo them, May. Then show me your footwork.

MEILIN I’d better. My getaway needs work.

DISCOVERING HER TALENT

The back row breaks up

MEILIN Vovô, your serious face is scaring the tambourine.

VOVÔ It hasn’t made a sound since you picked it up.

VOVÔ The whole room laughed. Did you hear them, May?

MEILIN I did. Was that the lesson?

AN INVITATION

By the studio door

VOVÔ That little café lets people tell jokes on Thursdays.

MEILIN What if they ask me to leave?

VOVÔ Then leave them laughing. May, you could do comedy for a living.

MEILIN Thursday, then. I’m saving you the wobbly chair.

PURSUING COMEDY

Five minutes at the microphone

MEILIN They put my name third. I can hear my knees knocking.

VOVÔ Good rhythm. Try opening with that.

MEILIN Good evening. My knees rehearsed a duet, but the left one forgot its part.

VOVÔ There it is, May. Listen to that room.

ON THE ROAD

A call after the late show

MEILIN Nobody laughed at the bus joke tonight.

VOVÔ Did you leave room for them?

MEILIN Vovô! I told the whole thing in one breath.

VOVÔ Try it again. I’ve got nowhere to be until breakfast.

PROFESSIONAL SUCCESS

The Sunday paper

MEILIN They spelled my name right this time!

VOVÔ I saw the profile. Sixty million US dollars in personal net worth. I kept a copy.

MEILIN From telling jokes. You were the first person who thought that was a job.

VOVÔ I still expect a ticket. A seat with four good legs this time.

THE SAME MEILIN

Breakfast at home

VOVÔ You flew home overnight just to steal my toast?

MEILIN Vovô, a woman worth sixty million dollars can afford jam.

VOVÔ But apparently not her own toast.

MEILIN Yours comes with better company.

THEIR OLD ROUTINE

Before the afternoon roda

VOVÔ You still leave your shoes in the doorway, May.

MEILIN They’re practicing their entrance.

VOVÔ Pick them up. Then you can practice yours.

MEILIN Yes, Vovô. Fame is very different in this house.

SOMETHING TO SHARE

A folded note

MEILIN Keep Saturday free for me?

VOVÔ Should I bring anything?

MEILIN Comfortable shoes. Your curious face comes free.

VOVÔ You’ve been rehearsing that answer.

A SECRET

On the way across town

VOVÔ You keep checking your pocket, May.

MEILIN Making sure your surprise hasn’t escaped.

VOVÔ If it fits in your pocket, why did we take the bus?

MEILIN That is exactly why you’re my favorite audience.

AT THE DOOR

One match to go

MEILIN Here we are. Close your eyes for one second, Vovô.

VOVÔ I heard keys. And that was more than one second.

MEILIN I’m a comedian. We call that timing.

VOVÔ All right, May. I’m listening.

A place for us

After the studio is damaged, Meilin opens her courtyard and indoor training studio to the family. She gives the players their keys.

Meilin places the last key in Coxa’s hand. “Why me?” Coxa asks. Meilin throws her arms open and yells, “Welcome to Roda!”
