The arcade movement draws on rhythm, balance, and low movement in Mestre Curtis Pierre’s New Orleans Kapoeira Angola practice.

Collect gold discs and enter the listed kick sequence in rhythm. K = kick, S = sweep. Each finished chain sends a pulse through nearby opponents.

    Collecting moves

    Walk through the gold disc near the start of each district on stages 1, 6, 11, 16, 21, 26, 31, 36, 41 and 46. Press Back to see your collection. On the F310, K means X (kick) and S means A (sweep). Your first disc unlocks Maré: press X, then A as the first attack finishes.

    Stage bosses

    Every route ends in a one-on-one fight. Every fifth opponent is a district chief.
