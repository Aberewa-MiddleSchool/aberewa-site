Capoeira Angola at Aberewa

Teachers can introduce a number pattern through a short clapping sequence or use a drawn movement pathway to discuss direction. Movement is optional, and quiz scores continue to measure the stated mathematics skill.

Our schoolwide approach is inspired by Mestre Curtis Pierre’s work in New Orleans, with attention to fluid movement, balance, flexibility, music, and cultural history.
